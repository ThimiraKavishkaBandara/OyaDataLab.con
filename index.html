How we work

Small scope, real result, no lock-in

A simple three-step engagement designed to prove value fast and hand you something you actually own.

01

Scope the bottleneck

A short call to find the highest-leverage thing to automate, what it’s costing you today, and what a good outcome looks like. You leave with a clear plan and a fixed quote — free, no obligation.

02

Build & iterate

We build in short cycles with something to look at early, so you’re never waiting months to see progress. You review, we adjust, we get it right against your real data.

03

Hand off & support

You get working, documented software that runs on its own — plus the source and a walkthrough so your team owns it. Ongoing support is available if you want it, never required.
